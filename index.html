Section 01 · Agenda

What this study answers

Before we spend money opening a new city, we want proof that people and businesses there actually need storage, and that we can win customers at a sensible cost.

1

Is there demand?

Enough people, professionals, students and businesses who would need storage or warehousing.

2

Can we run it profitably?

Cheap enough warehouse space, good roads, ports and rail, and room in the market against competitors.

3

Which city first?

Score both cities on the same checklist, compare them side by side and pick where to launch first.

      Section 05 · Recommendation

      What we recommend

      Ahmedabad scores higher on both checklists and is our recommended first city. Visakhapatnam stays on hold until the Ahmedabad test results are in.

      Ahmedabad · Launch first
      B2C: Go  ·  B2B: Go

      Household storage (B2C): confirm demand with the 30-day, ₹10,000 Google Ads test across the western residential belt (SG Highway, Satellite, Bopal, Shela and Gota), with prices in line with SafeStorage, the main competitor.

      Warehousing (B2B): offer flexible co-warehousing (no long lease, pay per pallet or per sq. ft.) to MSMEs and D2C brands, with space in the Changodar or Aslali belt.

      Visakhapatnam · On hold
      B2C: Conditional  ·  B2B: Hub only

      Household storage (B2C): search demand is small (about 110 searches a month). If we enter later, start asset-light with a partner warehouse and direct outreach to Navy families, IT relocation desks and colleges.

      Warehousing (B2B): enter only as a port-linked hub, and only after signing an anchor client in the Gajuwaka – Auto Nagar – Paravada corridor.

      Still to do before a final go / no-go (both cities): CAC tests, labour quotes from 3–5 local vendors, migration data (Census 2011 D-series), and an internal model for revenue per sq. ft., EBITDA and break-even.
